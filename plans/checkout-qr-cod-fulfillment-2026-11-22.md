# Unified QR/COD and POS fulfillment

Adds three methods to canonical checkout: Stripe, Techcombank QR, COD. QR uses merchant-provided bank details in runtime configuration, never in source. QR remains pending until authenticated bank evidence matches account/reference/currency/amount/time; buyer cannot mark paid. COD confirms an order with unpaid/to_collect, never produces a payment receipt. COD selection invalidates prepaid carrier quotes and calculates a stable fee including full COD collection value.

Server-owned carrier execution metadata records provider/account/shop/service/parcel/COD/environment. Before provider payment I/O, an immutable intent binds a domestic VND stock checkout to verified POS product/variation/warehouse mappings and a valid Vietnamese delivery phone. After verified prepaid settlement or COD acceptance, one commerce order/outbox is created using the existing order ID. International and POD orders are excluded. Stripe Sandbox export is separately gated, so test card payments do not masquerade as live prepaid dispatch.

Existing POS search/reference/lease/attempt safeguards are reused. A timeout only searches again; it never repeats an uncertain create. Stripe uses charged_by_card, QR transfer_money, COD has no prepaid value. POST is followed by GET and exact total/prepaid/COD readback checks. COD holds are consumed only on POS acknowledgement; prepaid stock already consumed by verified payment cannot be deducted a second time. Unexported COD cancellation releases its own hold. Schema changes are additive and idempotent.

After POS synchronization, existing carrier workflow reuses the selected execution owner and account. GHN dispatch requires runtime flag plus per-order snapshot flag. Existing Pancake GHN readback, unique tracking, attempted markers and progress model prevent duplicate bookings. Future carriers require their own verified adapter; no claim of support for an unimplemented provider. Buyer sees POS and shipment status separately from payment.
